COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions by Contractors

Everything you need to know about joining the FIX Partner Vendor Network in Bangladesh.

Is there any registration or enlistment fee?

No. Joining the FIX Vendor Network is 100% free. We do not charge any upfront onboarding, registration, or membership fees from technical contractors.

How and when do I get paid for completed jobs?

Disbursements are made bi-weekly (twice a month) directly via BEFTN bank transfer or bKash merchant payout, based on client sign-offs and digital work reports.

Can individual technicians or small teams apply?

Yes! We onboard individual certified master technicians, small specialized trade teams (2-5 people), as well as large registered engineering and cleaning companies.

How fast will my application be reviewed?

Our vendor onboarding desk reviews incoming applications within 24 to 48 business hours. You will receive an SMS and a verification phone call once reviewed.
